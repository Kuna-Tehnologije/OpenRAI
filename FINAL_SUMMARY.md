## ?? Kljuène Prednosti

### vs Pure LLM (GPT, Claude)
- ? **Persistent memory** (disk storage, ne samo context window)
- ? **Explicit knowledge graph** (ne samo implicitno u težinama)
- ? **Runtime goal alignment** (ne samo RLHF u trainingu)
- ? **Self-reflection** (meta-cognitive reasoning)
- ? **Parallel processing** (500× batch inference)
- ? **Explainable** (možeš tracati kroz semantic stack)

### vs Building LLM from Scratch
- ? **No training** (koristi Qwen/Llama)
- ? **Weeks not years** (brza integracija)
- ? **$0 training cost** (vs $100k+ GPU)
- ? **Focus on value** (cognition, ne language modeling)
