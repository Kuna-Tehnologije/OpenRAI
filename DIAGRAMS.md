## Concurrency Synchronization

```
Timeline:

t=0ms ? Main enqueues task_0 ? worker_0 wakes up
      ? Main enqueues task_1 ? worker_1 wakes up
      ? ... (all workers woken)
      ?
t=5ms ? Worker_0 executes task_0 (unlocked)
      ? Worker_1 executes task_1 (unlocked)
      ? Worker_2 waits on condition variable
      ? ... (parallel execution)
      ?
t=100ms ? Main calls wait_all()
        ? Main blocks on condition variable
        ?
t=150ms ? Worker_0 completes, notifies
        ? Worker_1 completes, notifies
        ? Main wakes up (check counter)
        ? Counter still > 0? Sleep again
        ?
t=200ms ? All workers done, counter = 0
        ? Main wakes up permanently
        ? Proceeds to PHASE 2
```

## Reasoning Decision Tree

```
Input: thought (string from short-term)

evaluate_thought()
?
?? calculate_importance()
?  ?
?  ?? thought.length() > 50? ? +0.3
?  ?? contains "important"?  ? +0.5
?  ?? contains "error"?      ? +0.3
?  ?? SCORE = min(1.0, sum)
?
?? is_relevant_for_output()
?  ?
?  ?? contains:
?     ?? "important"?     ? true
?     ?? "result"?        ? true
?     ?? "conclusion"?    ? true
?     (any match = output)
?
?? Return ReasoningResult
   ?? thought: string
   ?? importance_score: float
   ?? should_output: bool
   ?
   ?? If should_output:
   ?  ?? Print [OUTPUT]
   ?
   ?? Always:
      ?? add_to_long_term()
```

```

       ?  ?? Enqueue 10 tasks: 1ms
       ?  ?? Execute in parallel: 10-50ms (depends on CPU)
       ?  ?? wait_all() returns: 50ms total
       ?

       ?  ?? Sort 10 entries: 1ms
       ?  ?? Copy top 4: 1ms
       ?
52ms   ?? Phase 3 START
       ?  ?? For each of 4 entries:
       ?  ?  ?? Retrieve long-term: 1ms
       ?  ?  ?? Evaluate: 1ms
       ?  ?  ?? Add to long-term: 1ms
       ?  ?  ?? Total per entry: 3ms
       ?  ?? Total Phase 3: 12ms
       ?
64ms   ?? Phase 4 START
       ?  ?? Create GPU job: 1ms
       ?  ?? Enqueue (semaphore acquire): 1ms
       ?  ?? Total Phase 4: 2ms
       ?
66ms   ?? Phase 5 START
       ?  ?? sleep(3000ms)
       ?  ?? Idle for 2.934 seconds
       ?
3066ms ?? Iteration complete
        ?? Back to Phase 1 for next iteration
```
